How the Signal Engine Works

Five tightly integrated subsystems process every completed bar — from raw tick data to a validated, context-sorted cloud payload — with zero repainting and zero external price feeds.

🔒

SUBSYSTEM 01

New Bar Locking Engine

Every analysis cycle triggers exactly once per newly completed bar. A boolean state variable locks execution the moment a new candle opens, preventing duplicate signal generation within the same bar's lifespan. This is the foundational anti-repaint guarantee that makes MarubozuScan signals reliable for live trade execution.

TRIGGEROnNewBar() event flag
STATEBoolean lock per symbol × TF
REPAINTImpossible by design

SUBSYSTEM 02

Pure Candlestick Formula Scoring

The marubozu strength score is computed from first principles using a normalized body-to-range ratio: body% = (|close − open|) / (high − low). Signals only pass the formula gate when this ratio exceeds the configurable purity threshold (default 85%), ensuring only structurally dominant candles surface in the signal feed.

FORMULAbody / (high − low)
THRESHOLD≥ 0.85 body purity
OUTPUTNormalized score 0.00–1.00
📊

SUBSYSTEM 03

Institutional Volume Filter

Tick volume on each signal candle is measured against a 20-period exponential moving average. Signals with volume exceeding the threshold multiplier (default 1.5×) are classified as "Confirmed," indicating probable institutional order flow participation. Signals below the baseline are tagged "Pending" and displayed with reduced visual priority, keeping trader focus on conviction setups.

BASELINE20-bar EMA of tick volume
CONFIRMEDvol ≥ 1.5× baseline
SOURCEBroker tick volume (local)

SUBSYSTEM 04

Multi-Strategy Context Sorting

Each passing marubozu signal is assigned one of four Forex trading strategy contexts based on surrounding price structure analysis: Breakout (escape from consolidation), Continuation (trend-aligned), Reversal (key structure pivot), or False Breakout (trap rejection). This meta-layer allows traders to filter the live feed to their preferred strategy with a single tap.

CONTEXTS4 strategy classifications
ANALYSISATR range + swing structure
FILTERReal-time tab switching

SUBSYSTEM 05

Cloud Payload Compilation

After signal validation, the local engine serializes all qualifying signals into a compressed JSON payload with short-key encoding (e.g., 'tk' for ticker, 'pr' for price) to minimize bandwidth. The payload is POST-ed to a Supabase Edge Function within 200ms of bar close, where it is validated, timestamped, and served to all connected dashboard clients via the public API endpoint.

Frequently Asked Questions

Technical answers about how MarubozuScan's Forex signal engine detects and classifies candlestick patterns.

A marubozu is a full-bodied candlestick with little to no upper or lower wick, indicating that price opened at one extreme of the candle and closed at the other. In Forex trading, a bullish marubozu signals strong, uninterrupted buying pressure where bulls controlled price from open to close. A bearish marubozu signals the opposite — total seller domination. MarubozuScan requires a body-to-range ratio of at least 85% before classifying a candle as a qualifying marubozu pattern.
The volume filter compares each signal candle's tick volume against a rolling 20-period exponential moving average. Signals where volume exceeds 1.5× that baseline are tagged Confirmed, indicating likely participation from institutional-sized orders rather than retail noise. Signals between 1.0× and 1.5× are tagged Pending, and those below the average are tagged Below Average and displayed with reduced visual priority so traders can focus on high-conviction setups.
Four contexts are supported: Breakout — a marubozu that breaks and closes outside a prior ATR-defined consolidation range; Continuation — a candle aligned with the dominant trend structure confirmed by higher-high/higher-low analysis; Reversal — a pattern appearing at a key swing high or swing low with opposing momentum; and False Breakout — a rejection candle that traps breakout traders and signals a return to the prior range.

The engine's boolean lock prevents any recalculation once a signal fires for that bar. Because all formula inputs use the previous bar's closed values — never the live tick — signals are immutable once issued and will never appear or disappear on historical price data.
MarubozuScan monitors 28 Forex pairs including all major pairs (EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, USDCHF, AUDUSD, USDCAD, NZDUSD) plus the most-traded cross pairs (GBPJPY, EURJPY, EURGBP, AUDJPY, and more) across six timeframes: M1, M5, M15, M30, H1, H4, and D1. The local MetaTrader-based engine runs independently of third-party data subscriptions, using only the live feed from the connected broker.
