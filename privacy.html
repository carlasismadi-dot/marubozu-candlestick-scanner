03 Cookies & Web Beacons

A cookie is a small text file that is stored on your device by your web browser. Web beacons (sometimes called "tracking pixels" or "clear GIFs") are tiny, often invisible, graphic images used in combination with cookies to help us understand visitor behavior.

3.1 How marubozuscan.xyz Uses Cookies

MarubozuScan uses cookies to store information about visitors' preferences, to record user-specific information on which pages the user accesses or visits, and to customize web page content based on visitors' browser type or other information that the visitor sends via their browser. Specifically, our own first-party cookies may be used to:

  • Remember your preferred chart theme (e.g., dark mode vs. light mode display settings).
  • Store scanner filter configurations so that returning visitors do not need to re-configure their preferred candlestick pattern parameters every session.
  • Recognize repeat visits and measure aggregate site engagement for internal performance review.
  • Maintain session continuity, such as remembering your selected time-frame (e.g., daily, weekly, intraday) across page navigations.

3.2 Types of Cookies Used

Cookie Category Purpose Typical Duration
Strictly Necessary Essential for basic site functionality, such as layout rendering and security tokens. Session
Functional / Preference Remembers display settings, language, and chart configuration choices. Up to 12 months
Analytics / Performance Helps us understand how visitors interact with the scanner and which pages are most popular. Up to 24 months
Advertising Used by third-party vendors (see Section 4) to serve relevant advertisements. Varies by vendor (typically up to 13 months)

3.3 Managing Cookies

You are able to delete cookies or instruct your browser to delete or refuse cookies at any time via your browser settings. The procedure for managing cookies varies by browser; please consult your browser's "Help," "Settings," or "Privacy" menu for specific instructions. If you choose to disable cookies, some features of this Site — such as scanner filter persistence — may not function as intended.
